About OmniDish

A small team that loves food, and wants you to cook dishes from every corner of the world

Our story

Taste Around the World started as a university project. We wanted one place where anyone could pick a country and find its traditional recipes, with clear steps and ingredients

Today you can browse countries, explore categories, search for any dish, and watch video recipes.
