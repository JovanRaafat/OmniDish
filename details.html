Pizza

Italy

ingredients

  • Flour
  • 500g flour
  • 325 ml warm water
  • 7g yeast
  • 10 g salt
  • 2tbsp ovlive sauce
  • 200 g mozzarrella
  • oregano (option)
Measurements

Instructions

  • 1 In a bowl, mix flour, yeast, salt and water. Knead the dough for 10 minutes.
  • 2 Let it rise for 1-2 hours.
  • 3 Roll out the dough and add tomato sauce, cheese and toppings.
  • 4 Bake at 250°C (480°F) for 10-15 minutes.
